Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 July 2026  ·  Last updated: 15 July 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy is issued by Frontier Stride ("Frontier Stride", "we", "us", "our"), a sole proprietorship based in Gudivada, Krishna District, Andhra Pradesh, India. It applies to this website (frontierstride.com), and to the applications we develop and publish, including our School ERP software and our loan-management software (together, the "Services"). Frontier Stride is operated by Pragna Maddala, who also independently owns and operates Pragna Ayurvedic Life Sciences, a separate and unrelated business in the ayurvedic products and life sciences sector; the two businesses are operated independently and this information is provided for transparency of common ownership.

2. What information we collect

The information we collect depends on which Service you use.

  • Website visitors: we do not require you to create an account to browse this website. If you email us, we receive whatever information you choose to include in that email, such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message.
  • School ERP users: depending on the module in use, this may include student names, admission and roll numbers, attendance records, academic and examination records, fee payment records, and staff records such as names, contact details, and payroll information entered by the school.
  • Loan-management tool users: this may include applicant names, contact details, identification numbers required for lending compliance, loan application data, underwriting notes, disbursal and repayment records, and communications related to a loan file.
  • Automatically collected data: like most software, our applications and this website may collect limited technical data such as device type, operating system, IP address, and basic usage logs, for the purpose of keeping the Services secure and working correctly.

3. Who provides this information

For the School ERP and loan-management tools, the primary data controller is typically the institution (the school or the lending entity) that licenses and configures the software — not Frontier Stride directly. Students, parents, and loan applicants generally interact with that institution, and the institution is responsible for obtaining any consents required from the individuals whose data it enters into the Services. Frontier Stride processes this data to provide the software to that institution.

4. How we use information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Services;
  • To respond to enquiries sent to us directly, such as by email;
  • To maintain the security, integrity, and audit trail of records within the School ERP and loan-management tools;
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve the reliability of our software;
  • To comply with applicable law, including recordkeeping obligations that may apply to lending activity.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use student data or loan-applicant data collected through our Services for advertising purposes.

5. How information is stored and secured

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information handled by our Services against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Given the sensitivity of school records and financial records, access within our applications is intended to be limited to authorised users of the licensing institution, based on the roles that institution configures. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the Services to the licensing institution, and thereafter for as long as required to meet legal, accounting, or regulatory obligations — which may be longer for financial records subject to lending-related recordkeeping requirements. An institution may request deletion of its data subject to any retention obligations that apply to it.

7. Sharing of information

We do not share personal data with third parties except:

  • With service providers who help us operate the Services (for example, hosting providers), under obligations to protect that data;
  • Where required by law, regulation, or a valid legal process, including requirements that may apply to lending records;
  • With the licensing institution's own authorised personnel, as configured within the software;
  • With your consent, or at your direction.

8. Children's data

Our School ERP product is used by schools to manage student records, which may include data relating to children. This data is entered and controlled by the school, not collected by us directly from children. We rely on the school, as the data controller, to have the appropriate basis and parental notice for entering student information into the system. We do not knowingly use children's data for any purpose other than providing the School ERP functionality the school has configured.

9. Your rights

Depending on applicable law and your relationship to the Services (for example, as a parent, student, loan applicant, or website visitor), you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. Because much of the data in our School ERP and loan-management tools is entered and controlled by the licensing institution, we recommend directing such requests to that institution in the first instance; we will support the institution in responding where required. You can also contact us directly at the email below.

10. Governing law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of India, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, to the extent applicable.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above when we do. Material changes affecting how student or loan-applicant data is handled will be communicated to licensing institutions directly.
