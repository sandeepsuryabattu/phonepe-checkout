About the company

A small proprietorship, building for two specific problems.

Frontier Stride is not trying to be a general software vendor. It exists to build two things well: administrative software for schools, and operational software for lenders.

Why these two products

Institutions run on records. Most of those records are still fragile.

A school's academic year lives across attendance registers, fee receipt books, and a spreadsheet someone maintains from memory. A lender's loan book often lives across WhatsApp updates, paper files, and a collections list that's out of date by the time it's opened. Frontier Stride was started to replace that fragility with software that's boring in the best way — it just keeps working, and every record is where it should be.

We chose these two domains deliberately rather than building a generic app-development shop. School administration and lending operations both share a pattern: multiple people touching the same record over time, with real consequences if it's wrong. That's the problem we design around.
