What we build

Two product lines, one operating discipline

Both products come from the same premise: institutions run better when their record-keeping is accurate, auditable, and doesn't depend on spreadsheets held together by habit.

Education

School ERP

A single system for admissions, attendance, fee collection, timetables, and academic records — built for K-12 schools that are currently juggling registers, spreadsheets, and separate fee software.

  • Student admissions & records
  • Attendance and timetable management
  • Fee collection and receipts
  • Staff and payroll tracking
  • Parent-facing communication
Status — in development
Fintech

Loan Management Tools

Software for lenders and loan originators to manage a credit cycle end to end — application intake, underwriting checklists, disbursal tracking, and repayment schedules, with a clear audit trail at every stage.

  • Loan application & intake tracking
  • Underwriting checklists & approvals
  • Disbursal and repayment schedules
  • Collections and overdue tracking
  • Reporting for compliance reviews
Status — in development
Pricing

Pricing depends on institution size, modules selected, and support level. Across our School ERP and loan-management engagements, service fees typically range from ₹5,000 to ₹10,000. Get in touch for a quote tailored to your requirement.

How we work

Small team, direct accountability

01

Built by one team

Frontier Stride is a single, accountable proprietorship — not a reseller or an agency layer. The person who scopes your requirement is the same one who answers for the build.

02

Domain-first, not generic

We aren't building a general-purpose app toolkit. Every decision in the School ERP and the lending product is made against how schools and lenders actually operate day to day.

03

Data handled carefully

Both product lines touch sensitive records — student data on one side, financial data on the other. Our privacy and security approach is built around that, not added afterward.
