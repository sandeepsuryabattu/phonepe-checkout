Return Policy

Effective date: 15 July 2026  ·  Last updated: 15 July 2026

1. No physical goods

Frontier Stride ("Frontier Stride", "we", "us", "our") is a software development company based in Gudivada, Krishna District, Andhra Pradesh, India. We build and license the School ERP and loan-management software (together, the "Services"). We do not manufacture, sell, or ship any physical products. Because there is no physical item to send back, this Service does not support returns in the conventional retail sense.

2. What "return" means for our Services

Access to our Services is provided digitally, under a software licence or subscription. There is no physical unit, packaging, or courier shipment involved at any stage of delivery, and consequently there is no return shipping, restocking, or exchange process to describe.

3. Ending your use of a Service

If you no longer wish to use a Service you have licensed, you may cancel or request non-renewal of your subscription at any time by contacting us. This stops future access and billing but is not a "return" in the sense of sending anything back to us.

4. Fees already paid

Questions about fees already paid — including error charges or refund eligibility — are addressed separately in our Refund Policy, not this Return Policy, since they concern billing rather than the return of goods.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Return Policy from time to time, for example if we ever begin offering any physical merchandise or hardware alongside our software. We will revise the "Last updated" date above when we do.
